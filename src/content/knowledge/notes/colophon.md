This site uses the IBM Plex family (sans, serif, mono) plus Lumber Sans, an all-caps face he drew. It is built with Astro, hosted on Vercel, and designed in Figma. Icons include a set from Thiings.

He points at a personal collection of sites for inspiration, especially Adam Ho, Conor Davidson, Spencer Chang, Jacky Zhao (the background animation), and Solar Protocol.
