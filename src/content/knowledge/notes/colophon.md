This site uses the IBM Plex family (sans, serif, mono) plus Lumber Sans, an all-caps face he drew. It is built with Astro, with Svelte for the interactive parts, hosted on Vercel, and designed in Figma. The weather banner is drawn live in WebGL 2, the Ask Andrei guide runs on the Vercel AI SDK and AI Gateway, and the icons come from Pixelarticons.

He points at a personal collection of sites for inspiration, especially Adam Ho, Conor Davidson, Spencer Chang, Jacky Zhao (the background animation), and Solar Protocol.
