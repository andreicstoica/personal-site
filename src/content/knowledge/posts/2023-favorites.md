### My top articles to start the year (a little late 😉)

- Why The Age of American Progress Ended

- I generally agree with much more than the misguided 'it's time to build' doctrine much of tech seems to align with. 'Great Man' theory is not real!

- How Colleges Make More Money Than God By Giving It Away

- Illuminating piece on the financial logic of college tuitions.

- Nameless Feeling

- Lily and I have been picking up these themes repeatedly these last 6 weeks or so. How much of this do you see in yourself?

- No Good Alone

- Have found myself thinking about relationships as I’ve spent time with some older ‘millennial’ people of late (at work, friends of friends, etc.) and think of this piece.

- Why We Need New Words for Nature

- I was on a linguistics-theory reading kick last winter, and this piece stood out. While this piece focuses on how we speak about nature (or don’t), I found it interesting to extrapolate this point to other dominant themes in American life, like finance, education, politics, etc.
