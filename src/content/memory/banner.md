The banner at the top of every page is a live pixel landscape of an Oregon place, and visitors can change its weather, place, and time of day by clicking its sky, land, or sun, or the words of the caption under it.

The places are Painted Hills, Smith Rock, Mt. Hood, the Gorge, and Cannon Beach. It is drawn in WebGL with dithered color, and falls back to a still image when WebGL fails.

Its weather follows the visitor's own local weather (clear, cloudy, rain, or fog), and its light follows their sunrise and sunset (day, golden hour, or night). Rain brings lightning, and Mt. Hood's orchard sways in gusts of wind. Changes last until the page reloads.
