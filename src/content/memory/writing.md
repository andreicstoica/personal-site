Essays live on blog.andrei.bio, not as pages on this site.

Why I'm quitting my job (https://blog.andrei.bio/p/why-im-quitting-my-job): he describes leaving legacy-media product work because the days had no authorship. The piece moves from office ennui and status games to a childhood of making things, an NYU major built around making, then a turn toward the scaffolding around making (venture, history, policy). After Warner Bros. Discovery and NBCUniversal he gave notice. He wants to build opinionated products with people who have skin in the game, and he names NYC demo nights, protocols, and tech-community builders as the pull back toward making.

Other pieces he links from About, without a summary stored here: Netflix, WBD, and attention; Do people like slop?; Lessons on Living.

His undergraduate rationale was a short paper that bounded a self-named degree, written to open a colloquium with faculty rather than to win an argument. The public title he uses for the defense is Proposing a New Ethics for Human-Computer Interaction.
