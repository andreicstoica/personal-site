Outside of work he explores New York art exhibits, cooks, runs, plays tennis, reads a lot of Substacks, and makes coffee for friends.

Recently he has been thinking about essays on his blog: Netflix, WBD, and attention; Do people like slop?; Why I quit my job to build the future I want; and Lessons on Living, about growing up around people over 100 and how that changed his sense of time.

Micropages from About: Canon (people and works that shaped him), Colophon (how this site is built), and Fitness (running and the outdoors). He picked his own undergraduate classes, named the degree, and wrote about that in a thesis defense titled Proposing a New Ethics for Human-Computer Interaction.
