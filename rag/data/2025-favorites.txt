2025 Favorites
Books, YouTube, restaurants, and more!

Hi all,

I've gathered my favorite media and memories of the year. My absolute highlights are marked in bold and with a star.

## YouTube

Chris Young - a former 3 star Michelin chef who now makes a smart thermometer, and my favorite food/cooking YouTube videos to advertise it. The production value is insane and the topics lean into chemistry and physics.

Sharks Aren't Fish - by Hank Green

Cleo Abrams - an awesome science channel with a ton of access to ongoing research

Howtown - two video journalists who used to work at Vox. I particularly like their 'research showdowns' (aka debates) which are structured well - I learn a lot from them!

Extranet Shaquille - is the alt channel of a cooking YouTuber where he posts monologues on all sorts of topics. I love his pros and narration!

## Podcasts

Gear, Articles of Interest - the most interesting podcast series I've listened to in a looong time, talks about military clothing, outdoor clothing, how they are linked, and how clothing represents both from 1900-today. Grapples with the role of the US military and how it is portrayed (with clothing) to the US public.

Sarah Paine - full series on Dwarkesh Patel's podcast. I didn't know much about 'grand strategy' or how the military education system worked, but her talks about it are interesting. She's a great storyteller!

Maintaining Human Intelligence in the AI Era | David Krakauer (President of the Santa Fe Institute) on The Generalist.

Dialectic - overall my favorite podcast right now, constantly getting my reading cinematic universe to talk for 2-3 hours! Ordered favorites from this year alone: Tamara Winter - Tacit Trust & Caring Curiosity; Linus Lee - Engineering for Aliveness; Henrik Karlsson - Cultivating a Life that Fits; Nadia Asparouhova - Ideas That Infect; Geoffrey Litt - Software You Can Shape.
